# Build Embeddings From Scratch

Learn how Large Language Models understand language by building embeddings from scratch using TypeScript.

---

## Goal

Modern AI systems do not understand text directly.

Instead, they convert words into dense numerical vectors called **embeddings**.

In this project, we build every step ourselves to understand how this transformation works.

---

## What We Build

- One-Hot Encoding
- Bag of Words
- TF-IDF
- Co-occurrence Matrix
- Dense Embeddings
- Cosine Similarity
- Embedding Space Visualization

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this project, you'll understand:

- Why One-Hot Encoding fails
- How Bag of Words represents text
- Why TF-IDF improves word importance
- How co-occurrence captures context
- What embeddings are
- Why cosine similarity is used
- How Vector Databases and RAG use embeddings
